### What is Basic English?

Basic English is a simple form of the English language. It has only **850 words**. 

Charles Kay Ogden made this system in the year 1930. He made it to help all people in the world talk to one another with ease. 

### Why is it good for you?

* **It is small:** You do not need to learn thousands of words. You only need 850 words.
* **It is quick:** You can learn these words in a short time.
* **It is clear:** You can say almost everything with these simple words.
